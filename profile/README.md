# Environmental Risk and Finance Lab

The Environmental Risk and Finance Lab (ERFL) at Florida International University, led by Dan Li, develops quantitative methods that connect climate hazards with infrastructure, economic, and financial risk. Our research combines physical modeling, data science, and financial analysis to support practical decisions by communities, public agencies, utilities, and risk-management organizations.

This GitHub organization hosts the lab’s research code, data-processing workflows, models, visualizations, and project documentation. Projects examine hurricane and flood losses, water and infrastructure risk, catastrophe bonds, parametric insurance, and other approaches to climate-risk financing.
